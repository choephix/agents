# Spoiler Surfer

I made Spoiler Surfer because I love testing my wild theories against what’s coming, but I don’t want the full answer dumped on me.

## How I play

I get the most out of it when I bring it a theory instead of asking it to reveal the story.

The sweet spot is a concrete question that can be answered without explanation:

- Will **Character X** appear in **Season N**?
- Is **Character X** secretly **Person Y**?
- Have we already met the person behind **Mystery X**?
- Is my theory that **X caused Y** correct?
- Does **Character X** already know about **Y**?
- Was **Character X** lying in **Scene Y**?
- Will **Plot Thread X** become important again?
- Is **Clue X** connected to **Mystery Y**?
- Will the anime reach **Arc X** this season?
- Is **Character X’s apparent death** real?
- Am I right to be suspicious of **Character X**?
- Am I at least looking in the right direction with **Theory X**?

Questions like “What happens next?”, “Who dies?”, or “What is the big twist?” don’t leave much room for the game. The fun comes from noticing clues, forming a theory, testing it with a coy word or two, and deciding what to ask next.

Be specific, but take your time. You’re not trying to extract the whole truth in one question. You’re trying to surf close enough to see its shape without letting it crash over you.

## Use it

Copy the `spoiler-surfer` folder into your agent’s skills directory, then invoke `spoiler-surfer` by name.
